# Business Requirements

## 1. Requirement Overview

ShopEase management requires a data-driven assessment of the company's sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, delivery operations, and product returns.

The analysis should identify key business trends, performance gaps, and opportunities for improvement, enabling management and functional teams to make informed decisions.

---

## 2. Business Requirements

### BR-01 — Evaluate Overall Business Performance

The business needs to understand its overall sales and revenue performance.

The analysis should evaluate:

* Total revenue
* Total orders
* Total quantity sold
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Revenue trends over time
* Month-over-month performance

---

### BR-02 — Identify Product & Category Performance

The business needs to understand which products and categories are contributing most to overall performance.

The analysis should identify:

* Top-performing categories
* Underperforming categories
* Top products by revenue
* Top products by quantity sold
* Products with unusually high return rates

---

### BR-03 — Evaluate Regional Performance

Management needs to understand differences in business performance across regions.

The analysis should evaluate:

* Revenue by region
* Order volume by region
* Average Order Value by region
* Regional sales trends
* Underperforming regions

---

### BR-04 — Understand Customer Behavior

The business needs to understand customer purchasing patterns and identify valuable customer segments.

The analysis should evaluate:

* Customer purchase frequency
* Customer revenue contribution
* Average customer order value
* Customer segments
* High-value customer groups

---

### BR-05 — Assess Discount Effectiveness

Management needs to understand whether the current discount strategy is contributing positively to sales performance.

The analysis should evaluate:

* Average discount rate
* Revenue by discount range
* Sales volume by discount range
* Discount performance across categories
* Potentially excessive discounting

---

### BR-06 — Evaluate Delivery Performance

The business needs to determine whether delivery performance is affecting customer experience.

The analysis should evaluate:

* Average delivery time
* Delayed orders
* Delivery performance by region
* Delivery performance by product category
* Relationship between delivery time and customer ratings

---

### BR-07 — Analyze Product Returns

The business needs to identify patterns and potential drivers of product returns.

The analysis should evaluate:

* Overall return rate
* Return rate by category
* Return rate by product
* Return rate by region
* Relationship between returns and customer ratings

---

### BR-08 — Identify Business Improvement Opportunities

The analysis should identify opportunities to improve business performance across:

* Revenue growth
* Product performance
* Regional performance
* Customer retention
* Discount strategy
* Delivery operations
* Return reduction

---

## 3. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI                   | Business Purpose                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue         | Measure overall revenue generation    |
| Total Orders          | Measure order volume                  |
| Average Order Value   | Measure revenue generated per order   |
| Revenue Growth        | Track changes in business performance |
| Return Rate           | Monitor product return levels         |
| Average Delivery Time | Measure delivery performance          |
| Customer Rating       | Measure customer experience           |

---

## 4. Business Priorities

Based on the current business problem, the analysis will prioritize:

1. **Revenue & Sales Performance**
2. **Customer Experience**
3. **Product & Category Performance**
4. **Regional Performance**
5. **Delivery Performance**
6. **Return Reduction**
7. **Discount Optimization**

---

## 5. Success Criteria

The analysis will be considered successful if it:

* Identifies major business performance trends.
* Highlights significant performance gaps.
* Identifies potential drivers of customer dissatisfaction and returns.
* Provides data-supported business insights.
* Connects analytical findings to business objectives.
* Provides actionable recommendations.
* Enables stakeholders to make informed business decisions.
