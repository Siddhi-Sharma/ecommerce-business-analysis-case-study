# Functional Requirements

## 1. Purpose

The functional requirements define the capabilities that the business analysis and reporting solution must provide to address ShopEase's business requirements.

The solution should allow stakeholders to explore business performance, identify trends, investigate problem areas, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 2. Functional Requirements

### FR-01 — Sales Performance Analysis

The solution must allow users to:

* View total revenue.
* View total orders.
* View total quantity sold.
* Calculate Average Order Value (AOV).
* Analyze revenue trends over time.
* Compare sales performance across different periods.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-01

---

### FR-02 — Product & Category Analysis

The solution must allow users to:

* Compare revenue across product categories.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Identify underperforming products.
* Compare quantity sold across categories.
* Analyze product return rates.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-02

---

### FR-03 — Regional Analysis

The solution must allow users to:

* Compare revenue across regions.
* Compare order volume across regions.
* Compare Average Order Value across regions.
* Identify high- and low-performing regions.
* Analyze regional sales trends.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-03

---

### FR-04 — Customer Analysis

The solution must allow users to:

* Segment customers based on purchasing behavior.
* Calculate customer revenue contribution.
* Analyze purchase frequency.
* Identify high-value customer segments.
* Compare Average Order Value across customer segments.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-04

---

### FR-05 — Discount Analysis

The solution must allow users to:

* Calculate discount percentages.
* Group transactions into discount ranges.
* Compare revenue across discount ranges.
* Compare sales volume across discount ranges.
* Analyze discount performance by product category.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-05

---

### FR-06 — Delivery Performance Analysis

The solution must allow users to:

* Calculate average delivery time.
* Identify delayed orders.
* Compare delivery performance across regions.
* Compare delivery performance across product categories.
* Analyze the relationship between delivery time and customer ratings.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-06

---

### FR-07 — Return Analysis

The solution must allow users to:

* Calculate overall return rate.
* Calculate return rate by product category.
* Calculate return rate by product.
* Compare return rates across regions.
* Analyze the relationship between returns and customer ratings.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-07

---

### FR-08 — Dashboard & Reporting

The solution must provide an interactive business dashboard containing:

* Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Sales trends
* Product/category performance
* Regional performance
* Customer insights
* Delivery performance
* Return analysis

Users should be able to filter relevant information by dimensions such as:

* Date
* Region
* Product category
* Product
* Customer segment

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-01 to BR-08

---

### FR-09 — Business Insights

The analysis should translate quantitative findings into business insights.

Each major insight should:

1. Identify the observed trend or issue.
2. Provide supporting data.
3. Explain the potential business implication.
4. Identify the relevant business area.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-08

---

### FR-10 — Recommendations

The final analysis must provide actionable recommendations based on identified business issues and opportunities.

Recommendations should:

* Be supported by analysis.
* Address a specific business problem.
* Identify the relevant stakeholder/team.
* Explain the expected business benefit.

**Related Business Requirement:** BR-08
