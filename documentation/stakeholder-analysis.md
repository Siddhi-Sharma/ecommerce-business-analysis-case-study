# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify the key stakeholders involved in ShopEase's business performance and understand their information needs, responsibilities, and expectations from the analysis.

---

## 2. Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder           | Role / Interest                                      | Key Information Needed                                                       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business Management   | Overall business performance and strategic decisions | Revenue, growth, profitability indicators, regional and category performance |
| Sales Team            | Sales performance and revenue growth                 | Product performance, regional sales, customer segments, sales trends         |
| Marketing Team        | Customer acquisition and retention                   | Customer segments, purchasing behavior, discount effectiveness               |
| Operations Team       | Order fulfillment and delivery performance           | Delivery times, delayed orders, regional operational performance             |
| Customer Support Team | Customer satisfaction and complaints                 | Customer ratings, returns, delivery issues, customer concerns                |
| Product Team          | Product performance and customer demand              | Product sales, returns, ratings, category trends                             |
| Finance Team          | Revenue and financial performance                    | Revenue, discounts, order value, sales trends                                |

---

## 3. Stakeholder Priorities

### Business Management

**Primary priority:** Improve overall business performance.

Management requires a high-level view of revenue, sales trends, customer behavior, and areas requiring improvement.

### Sales Team

**Primary priority:** Increase sales and identify underperforming areas.

The sales team needs information about product, category, and regional performance to identify opportunities.

### Marketing Team

**Primary priority:** Improve customer acquisition and retention.

The marketing team needs customer segmentation and purchasing behavior insights to support targeted campaigns.

### Operations Team

**Primary priority:** Improve order fulfillment and delivery performance.

The operations team needs to identify areas where delivery delays or operational issues may be affecting customer experience.

### Customer Support Team

**Primary priority:** Improve customer satisfaction.

The customer support team needs visibility into ratings, returns, delivery issues, and other customer experience indicators.

### Product Team

**Primary priority:** Improve product performance and customer experience.

The product team needs information about product demand, ratings, and return patterns.

### Finance Team

**Primary priority:** Monitor revenue and the financial impact of sales strategies.

The finance team requires information about revenue, discounts, order values, and sales trends.

---

## 4. Stakeholder Expectations

The stakeholders expect the analysis to:

* Provide accurate and understandable business insights.
* Identify major performance trends and problem areas.
* Highlight opportunities for improvement.
* Support data-driven decision-making.
* Provide actionable recommendations.
* Present findings in a clear and accessible format.

---

## 5. Communication & Reporting

The final analysis will communicate findings through:

* Business analysis documentation
* Key performance indicators
* Data visualizations
* Interactive dashboard
* Executive-level summary
* Actionable recommendations
