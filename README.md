# 🛒 E-commerce Business Analysis Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a complete **Business Analysis case study** for a fictional e-commerce company, **ShopEase**.

The company has experienced inconsistent business performance across different product categories and regions. Management also wants to better understand customer experience, delivery performance, and product returns.

The objective of this case study is to analyze the available business data, identify key business problems and opportunities, and provide **data-driven recommendations** to support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

ShopEase has observed differences in sales performance across products and regions, along with challenges related to customer experience, delivery times, and product returns.

Management wants to understand:

* What factors are driving revenue?
* Which product categories and regions are performing well?
* Where are the major areas of underperformance?
* How do discounts affect sales performance?
* Does delivery performance influence customer satisfaction?
* Which customer segments contribute the most value?
* What factors may be associated with product returns?
* What actions can the business take to improve performance?

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to:

1. Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance.
2. Identify high- and low-performing products and regions.
3. Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
4. Evaluate the relationship between discounts and sales.
5. Analyze delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
6. Identify patterns in product returns.
7. Identify potential business improvement opportunities.
8. Provide actionable recommendations to management.

## 📋 Business Requirements

Based on the business problem, objectives, and stakeholder needs, the following business requirements have been identified:

### BR-01: Sales & Revenue Performance

Analyze sales and revenue trends over time to understand overall business performance and identify periods of growth or decline.

### BR-02: Product & Category Performance

Identify high- and low-performing products and categories based on sales, revenue, and order volume.

### BR-03: Regional Performance

Evaluate sales and customer distribution across different regions to identify high-performing and underperforming markets.

### BR-04: Customer Purchasing Behavior

Analyze customer purchasing patterns, order frequency, and customer value to identify important customer segments and potential retention opportunities.

### BR-05: Discount & Pricing Analysis

Evaluate the relationship between discounts, order value, and sales performance to determine whether discounting strategies are effective.

### BR-06: Delivery Performance

Measure delivery times and compare actual delivery dates with estimated delivery dates to identify delays and operational issues.

### BR-07: Customer Satisfaction

Analyze customer review scores and investigate whether delivery performance is associated with customer satisfaction.

### BR-08: Returns Analysis

Identify patterns in product returns across categories, products, and regions to understand potential areas of concern.

### BR-09: Business Recommendations

Translate analytical findings into actionable recommendations that can improve revenue performance, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

## 📊 Data Understanding

The analysis uses the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset, which contains information about orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and geographic locations.

### Datasets Used

| Dataset                                 | Purpose                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `olist_orders_dataset.csv`              | Order status, purchase dates, delivery dates, and estimated delivery dates |
| `olist_order_items_dataset.csv`         | Products purchased, sellers, prices, and freight values                    |
| `olist_customers_dataset.csv`           | Customer identifiers and geographic information                            |
| `olist_products_dataset.csv`            | Product details and product categories                                     |
| `olist_order_payments_dataset.csv`      | Payment methods, installments, and payment values                          |
| `olist_order_reviews_dataset.csv`       | Customer review scores and review information                              |
| `olist_sellers_dataset.csv`             | Seller identifiers and geographic information                              |
| `olist_geolocation_dataset.csv`         | Brazilian ZIP-code geographic information                                  |
| `product_category_name_translation.csv` | Portuguese-to-English product category translations                        |

### Data Quality Checks

Before analysis, the datasets will be reviewed for:

* Missing values
* Duplicate records
* Data types and formatting issues
* Invalid or inconsistent dates
* Relationships between datasets
* Potential data quality issues

The relevant datasets will then be cleaned and joined to create an analysis-ready dataset for further business analysis.
