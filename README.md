# 🛒 E-commerce Business Analysis Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a complete **Business Analysis case study** for a fictional e-commerce company, **ShopEase**.

The company has experienced inconsistent business performance across different product categories and regions. Management also wants to better understand customer experience, delivery performance, and product returns.

The objective of this case study is to analyze the available business data, identify key business problems and opportunities, and provide **data-driven recommendations** to support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

ShopEase has observed differences in sales performance across products and regions, along with challenges related to customer experience, delivery times, and product returns.

Management wants to understand:

* What factors are driving revenue?
* Which product categories and regions are performing well?
* Where are the major areas of underperformance?
* How do discounts affect sales performance?
* Does delivery performance influence customer satisfaction?
* Which customer segments contribute the most value?
* What factors may be associated with product returns?
* What actions can the business take to improve performance?

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to:

1. Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance.
2. Identify high- and low-performing products and regions.
3. Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
4. Evaluate the relationship between discounts and sales.
5. Analyze delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
6. Identify patterns in product returns.
7. Identify potential business improvement opportunities.
8. Provide actionable recommendations to management.

---

## 👥 Stakeholder Analysis

The key stakeholders involved in the business problem are:

| Stakeholder              | Role                            | Key Needs                                                          |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Senior Management        | Strategic decision-making       | Overall business performance, revenue growth, and profitability    |
| Sales & Marketing Team   | Sales and customer growth       | Product performance, customer behavior, and regional sales trends  |
| Operations Team          | Order and delivery management   | Delivery performance, delays, and operational issues               |
| Customer Experience Team | Customer satisfaction           | Reviews, ratings, delivery experience, and customer concerns       |
| Product Team             | Product and category management | Category performance, product demand, and underperforming products |
| Finance Team             | Financial monitoring            | Revenue, payment trends, order value, and financial performance    |

### Stakeholder Priorities

The analysis will prioritize business areas that have the greatest potential impact on:

* Revenue growth
* Customer satisfaction
* Operational efficiency
* Product and category performance
* Regional performance
* Business decision-making


## 📊 Key Business Questions

The analysis will answer the following questions:

### Sales & Revenue

* Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
* Which regions contribute the most to sales?
* How does revenue change over time?
* Which areas are underperforming?

### Customers

* Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
* What purchasing patterns can be identified?
* Which customers or segments may require retention strategies?

### Discounts

* Are higher discounts associated with higher sales?
* Which categories receive the highest discounts?
* Is the current discount strategy effective?

### Customer Experience

* Does delivery time affect customer ratings?
* Which regions have longer delivery times?
* Are delayed orders associated with lower satisfaction?

### Returns

* Which products or categories have the highest return rates?
* Are certain regions more affected by returns?
* What patterns can be identified among returned orders?
